⚗️ Soul Rules of Teyvat

⚠️ These rules only apply inside the False Sky (Teyvat) -- Mostly Rules set by first Descenders. Outside the false sky — no such rules exist.
The Moon's Reflection Space has its own separate rules (exception).

1
A living entity is made of soul/authority/power + a body. These two together define a living being.
2
A part of a soul cannot exist without a body. Soul must always be anchored in a living vessel.
3
A soul can be divided into parts and distributed across multiple bodies simultaneously.
4
A body can contain multiple souls at the same time (overlapping soul hosting).
5
If a partial soul is in a body and that body dies, the soul-part merges with the surviving body's soul.
6
If the entire soul of a person cannot find any living body to inhabit → that entity is considered truly dead.

📜 Pre-History of Teyvat

Ancient Era · Dragon Age
Nibelung, the Dragon King
Ruled Teyvat by creating three moons to watch over the life-cycles of Teyvat — governing life, death, space, time, and reason. Grew bored and left to travel the universe in search of power.
Interregnum · Guest Era
Heavenly Principles Arrives
HP came to Teyvat while Nibelung was away. The three moon sisters welcomed HP as a guest. Initially no ill intent — HP simply watched over Teyvat. Teyvat at this time had diverse species: Hyperboreans, Angels, Dragonkin, Faes, and more.
HP Rule Begins
HP's Fear & the Strict Order
HP began to fear those who seek power through greed, afraid of losing authority. Created a rule-based order — copied the moon sisters' system to make Shades, implemented strict laws, created relatively weak human race and annihilated any civilization threatening his authority via Divine Nails. Rules were fair but penalties were extreme.
Moon Sisters' Debate
The Three Sisters Disagree
The moon sisters debated whether to oppose HP:

🌙 Iridescent Moon — Opposed HP
🌙 Frost Moon — Neutral
🌙 Eternal Moon — Aligned with HP

Eventually all three agreed: HP's rule of Teyvat was wrong.
The War Begins
Nibelung Returns with Abyss Power
Before the moon sisters could act, Nibelung returned — wielding the power of the Abyss from beyond the world. Enraged that an alien (HP) was ruling his world, and that the moon sisters had "betrayed" him by welcoming HP, he imprisoned all three moon sisters' souls in the Moon's Reflection — a time-reversed jail from which none can escape once entered.
HP vs Nibelung War
The Moons Destroyed
HP used most of his power to defeat Nibelung. In the war, all three physical satellite moons were destroyed. Only the Frost Moon's corpse survived mostly intact. The other moons were shattered — only fragments remained. Past The question of how these fragments regained power is answered in [4].
Khanriah Age
Rerir & Tholindis Enter the Reflection
Thousands of years later, Rerir and Tholindis entered the Moon's Reflection and got trapped. Rerir's body shattered into pieces from incompatibility with the Moon's Reflection space— but survived due to an immortality curse from Ronova-shade of death. (The Wild Hunt - abyss revives rerir due to vengence) He later re-entered [thrown/sealed into the moon's reflection with the help of Dainslef etc.] and was barely compatible with the space due to the Khuvakhi he absorbed from Nod Krai[Wild Hunt]. Tholindis's mortal body died from lethal energy concentrations. Their soul/authority was sealed by the space and they both are separated by 'time' and cannot meet each other eternally [sad].

🪞 The Moon's Reflection Space

WHAT IT IS

A time-reversed jail space created by Nibelung to imprison the three moon sisters' souls/authority. Walking in any direction causes time to flow backwards. Anyone who enters will loop back to the moment of their imprisonment.

WHO IS IMPRISONED

The jail only fully traps soul/authority/power. Mortals who enter get destroyed by high energy concentrations (Tholindis died). Immortals survive but their powers are stripped (Rerir shattered but survived via curse).

Key Insight: "Moon's Reflection Space / Jail / Space" — all terms used interchangeably to refer to the time-reversed space Bina entered in the previous Archon Quest.

Soul can travel through time itself to fulfill this rule.

🧪 Sandrone's Formula — Changing Fate

The Goal: Teyvat operates like a simulation cycle — defined by rules from Nibelung & HP. These rules create fate (events that are bound to happen, cannot be changed). Sandrone doesn't just want to predict fate (like Rene Petrichor did for Fontaine's prophecy) — she wants to change it, specifically to tie Bina back to Teyvat and create the moment of her return.

🌀
Outside Variables (Descenders)
Introducing variables from outside the system — i.e., Descenders like the Traveller — who are not bound by Teyvat's rules. They break the determinism.
🌳
The Tree Model (Seal of Chymical Marriage)
Pathway discovered by Rene Petrichor: a circle of 4 orthants (Memories, Wishes, Soul, Persona) + Tree of Emanation (Rule, Sustain, Destroy). Albedo confirmed this — so Sandrone reluctantly used it despite not believing in soul/persona.
📚
Historical Record Nodes
The tree needs nodes from recorded history: world, space-time, civilization records. Specifically the Hyperborean civilization that worshipped the moon — these are the variable nodes pointing to primordial Light power.
⚙️
Computing Power (Wanderer's Core)
Processing enormous historical + real-time data requires massive computing power. Sandrone discovered Alain Guillotine (her creator/father) failed to change fate because he lacked this. Solution: Wanderer's core, derived from Irminsul.
🔗
Bina's Link (Past → Present)
For Bina to reappear, there must be a verified link between her past self and present self. The formula calculates the exact moment this link can be activated without breaking Teyvat's quantum rules.
🎲
Generational Luck (The Miracle)
Even with all the above, a miracle element — generational luck — is required. This is the intangible final ingredient that Sandrone's formula accounts for but cannot force.
Nefer found Bina's plans through her memories and relayed it to Sandrone, giving Sandrone the final piece needed to complete the formula. At the cost of protecting the real formula from being destroyed by him, Sandrone 'dies' at the hands of dottore.
