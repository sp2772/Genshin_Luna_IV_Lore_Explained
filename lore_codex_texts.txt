MODULE 1 — SOUL RULES OF TEYVAT
================================================================================
!!! These rules only apply inside the False Sky (Teyvat) --> Mostly Rules set by first Descenders. Outside the false sky — no such rules exist.
The Moon's Reflection Space has its own separate rules (exception).

Rule 1:
A living entity is made of soul/authority/power + a body. These two together define a living being.

Rule 2:
A part of a soul cannot exist without a body. Soul must always be anchored in a living vessel.

Rule 3:
A soul can be divided into parts and distributed across multiple bodies simultaneously.

Rule 4:
A body can contain multiple souls at the same time (overlapping soul hosting).

Rule 5:
If a partial soul is in a body and that body dies, the soul-part merges with the surviving body's soul.

Rule 6:
If the entire soul of a person cannot find any living body to inhabit → that entity is considered truly dead.

MODULE 2 — PRE-HISTORY OF TEYVAT
================================================================================

[Ancient Era · Dragon Age]
Nibuleng, the Dragon King
Ruled Teyvat by creating three moons to watch over the life-cycles of Teyvat — governing life, death, space, time, and reason. Grew bored and left to travel the universe in search of power.

[Interregnum · Guest Era]
Heavenly Principles Arrives
HP came to Teyvat while Nibuleng was away. The three moon sisters welcomed HP as a guest. Initially no ill intent — HP simply watched over Teyvat. Teyvat at this time had diverse species: Hyperboreans, Angels, Dragonkin, Faes, and more.

[HP Rule Begins]
HP's Fear & the Strict Order
HP began to fear those who seek power through greed, afraid of losing authority. Created a rule-based order — copied the moon sisters' system to make Shades, implemented strict laws, and annihilated any civilization threatening his authority via Divine Nails. Rules were fair but penalties were extreme.

[Moon Sisters' Debate]
The Three Sisters Disagree
The moon sisters debated whether to oppose HP:
- Iridescent Moon — Opposed HP
- Frost Moon — Neutral
- Eternal Moon — Aligned with HP
Eventually all three agreed: HP's rule of Teyvat was wrong.

[The War Begins]
Nibuleng Returns with Abyss Power
Before the moon sisters could act, Nibuleng returned — wielding the power of the Abyss from beyond the world. Enraged that an alien (HP) was ruling his world, and that the moon sisters had "betrayed" him by welcoming HP, he imprisoned all three moon sisters' souls in the Moon's Reflection — a time-reversed jail from which none can escape once entered.

[HP vs Nibuleng War]
The Moons Destroyed
HP used most of his power to defeat Nibuleng. In the war, all three physical satellite moons were destroyed. Only the Frost Moon's corpse survived mostly intact. The other moons were shattered — only fragments remained. [Past] The question of how these fragments regained power is answered in [4].

[Khanriah Age]
Rerir & Tholindis Enter the Reflection
Thousands of years later, Rerir and Tholindis entered the Moon's Reflection and got trapped. Rerir's body shattered into pieces from incompatibility — but survived due to an immortality curse (the Wild Hunt). He later re-entered using Khuvakhi absorbed from Nod Krai. Tholindis's mortal body died from lethal energy concentrations. Their soul/authority was stripped by the space.

MODULE 4 — THE MOON'S REFLECTION SPACE
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS:
A time-reversed jail space created by Nibuleng to imprison the three moon sisters' souls/authority. Walking in any direction causes time to flow backwards. Anyone who enters will loop back to the moment of their imprisonment.

WHO IS IMPRISONED:
The jail only fully traps soul/authority/power. Mortals who enter get destroyed by high energy concentrations (Tholindis died). Immortals survive but their powers are stripped (Rerir shattered but survived via curse).

Key Insight: "Moon's Reflection Space / Jail / Space" — all terms used interchangeably to refer to the time-reversed space Bina entered in the previous Archon Quest.

MODULE 5 — BINA'S JOURNEY THROUGH THE REFLECTION SPACE
================================================================================

[PRESENT → PAST]
Time flows backwards in the reflection space. Reading these steps = going further into the past.

Step 0 — Trigger Event:
Bina enters the Moon's Reflection in desperation to save the Traveller from Dottore's tri-lunar powers. (Dottore later creates an artificial moon fragment, but that's a separate development.)

Step 1 — Discovery:
Bina realizes time flows backwards inside the space. She tries to interact with the past using her Khuvakhi powers to warn friends about doomsday (Dottore getting all 3 moons).

Step 2 — Khuvakhi Limit Found:
Bina learns: using too much Khuvakhi to interact with the past forces her back into the space. (Proved via the Barbeloth Mage Astrology Card incident, and the Traveller turning back when she touched their shoulder.)

Step 3 — Writing on the Wall:
Bina writes using Khuvakhi in the Traveller's past room, hoping they'd remember: "open the windows, let moonlight in, I can write on the walls using moonlight." She wrote everything — her survival, her situation — on the wall. The writing was always there but unnoticed until Nefer & Flins found it in the present (after the Moon Prayer incident).

Step 4 — !!! Critical Action:
With little power remaining, Bina cuts a part of her soul and leaves it with the Traveller — on their very first meeting day — as Luonnatar. This soul-fragment is the key to everything that follows.

Step 5 — Revelation:
Bina realizes why she could exit the reflection space temporarily — the memory bubble tunnels always led to the Traveller's location. This is because she left a part of her soul (Luonnatar) with the Traveller, and souls attract across time.

Step 6 — Rerir & Tholindis Encountered:
Bina sees Rerir and Tholindis and confirms the rule: the Reflection space only traps authority/soul. Rerir's Abyssal powers are gone, but he survives due to his immortality curse. Tholindis's mortal body died from the energy.

Step 7 — Khuvakhi Revelation:
Bina realizes: Khuvakhi is her own innate personal power/authority. She is not just a person — she is, essentially, a Fourth Moon.

Step 8 — Ancient Past Reached:
Continuing deeper through the reflection space, Bina travels far enough back to reach the moment Nibuleng originally imprisoned the three moon sisters.

Step 9 — !!! [4] The Gamble:
Bina absorbs all three moon sisters' authority/power into her own body. The moon sisters can now "die" inside the jail (as their souls/authority left), just like Tholindis died when her body had no moon power to sustain it. Bina's body is now carrying trilunar authority — albeit unstably.

Step 10 — Promise Made:
Bina promises the moon sisters she will return their powers to their physical moon bodies/fragments once she escapes. This explains how the moon fragments later regained some consciousness and power.

Step 11 — Death in the Space:
Bina dies inside the reflection space, holding the trilunar authority. Her body sacrifices itself. But since a part of her soul — Luonnatar — exists in the present, her soul escapes the reflection space (soul travels through time to rejoin its other half) and magnetically attaches to Luonnatar at one point in time when a certain condition is met: when the moon is brought inside teyvat's skies for the soul rules to work.

Soul Travel Logic: The jail traps soul/authority only while you are inside the space. When Bina's body dies while a part of her soul exists outside (as Luonnatar in the present), her soul escapes by following the quantum rule — two soul-parts reunite when one body dies. Soul can travel through time itself to fulfill this rule.

MODULE 6 — SANDRONE'S FORMULA — CHANGING FATE
================================================================================

The Goal: Teyvat operates like a simulation cycle — defined by rules from Nibuleng & HP. These rules create fate (events that are bound to happen, cannot be changed). Sandrone doesn't just want to predict fate (like Rene Petrichor did for Fontaine's prophecy) — she wants to change it, specifically to tie Bina back to Teyvat and create the moment of her return.

Formula Ingredient 1 — Outside Variables (Descenders):
Introducing variables from outside the system — i.e., Descenders like the Traveller — who are not bound by Teyvat's rules. They break the determinism.

Formula Ingredient 2 — The Tree Model (Seal of Chymical Marriage):
Pathway discovered by Rene Petrichor: a circle of 4 orthants (Memories, Wishes, Soul, Persona) + Tree of Emanation (Rule, Sustain, Destroy). Albedo confirmed this — so Sandrone reluctantly used it despite not believing in soul/persona.

Formula Ingredient 3 — Historical Record Nodes:
The tree needs nodes from recorded history: world, space-time, civilization records. Specifically the Hyperborean civilization that worshipped the moon — these are the variable nodes pointing to primordial Light power.

Formula Ingredient 4 — Computing Power (Wanderer's Core):
Processing enormous historical + real-time data requires massive computing power. Sandrone discovered Alain Guillotine (her creator/father) failed to change fate because he lacked this. Solution: Wanderer's core, derived from Irminsul.

Formula Ingredient 5 — Bina's Link (Past → Present):
For Bina to reappear, there must be a verified link between her past self and present self. The formula calculates the exact moment this link can be activated without breaking Teyvat's quantum rules.

Formula Ingredient 6 — Generational Luck (The Miracle):
Even with all the above, a miracle element — generational luck — is required. This is the intangible final ingredient that Sandrone's formula accounts for but cannot force.

Note: Nefer found Bina's plans through her memories and relayed it to Sandrone, giving Sandrone the final piece needed to complete the formula.
At the cost of protecting the real formula from being destroyed by him, Sandrone 'dies' at the hands of dottore.

MODULE 10 — TRAVELLER LORE — THE SPACESHIP THEORY
================================================================================

- The spaceship entered Teyvat's skies long ago — slowed down, then accelerated again. Logs show it orbited Teyvat for thousands of years before finally crashing into Nod Krai.

- The Sibling left the spaceship earlier — possibly when it first grazed Teyvat (during the age of Khanriah). The Traveller was asleep in a hibernation chamber this entire time while the ship orbited — and remained asleep even after the crash.

- The Sibling somehow teleported to Khanriah without memories, slowly recovered them there, but could not locate the spaceship (possibly because it was still orbiting in outer space).

- Direidyth shows the Sibling a key to the Traveller's hibernation chamber — obtained from a future Traveller. This gave the Sibling proof the Traveller was alive and the ship was in Teyvat.

- The Sibling found the crashed ship (unknown how), rescued the Traveller, and they both tried to escape Teyvat — instead of saving Khanriah as their Savior. They encountered Asmoday (the Unknown God) blocking their escape.

- Dottore Theory: The NPC phantoms near the spaceship were all Hyperboreans/ancient age. If the ship crashed at Khanriah's time, the timeline doesn't fit. Columbina's powers showed the ship was abandoned for many years. Theory: Dottore manipulated the past records by introducing variables through phantoms in the past, fabricating history to set up his grand plan of obtaining all three moons. The Wild Hunt phantoms may all be falsified.

The ship likely crashed after the Hyperborean age and some years before the Cataclysm. After the Cataclysm, HP hid the spaceship behind the Frost Moon corpse.
